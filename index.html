What counts as use?

A paper qualifies when its text, figures, or accompanying research code documents how the authors used ANDES for their own work. Each entry links to the evidence. A background mention or citation alone is insufficient.

Map markers show author affiliations at publication. A paper with several institutions counts once in the index. We link preprints to their published versions when identified.
