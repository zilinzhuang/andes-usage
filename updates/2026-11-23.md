# Literature update for November 23, 2026

The search added 13 previously missed studies and corrected one existing record. The atlas now contains 133 studies and 110 author institutions across 20 countries or regions. Eight additions came from the existing candidate list. The other five were identified separately. No paper first made public from November 18 through November 23 was verified for inclusion during this review. The latest addition is an alphaXiv preprint posted on November 14.

## Added papers

### JAX-EMT: A Differentiable, High-Performance Electromagnetic-Transient Platform for Stability Analysis and Grid-Interactive Power Electronics Design and Optimization

2026-11-14 · Preprint · alphaXiv

The authors compared 24 electromechanical modes from JAX-EMT with ANDES 2.0 results on a 243-bus WECC system, reporting differences in modal frequencies and damping ratios. [Section IV-A, Modeling and Simulation Accuracy, PDF p. 7. Figure 3(d)–(e), p. 8](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.jax-emt-differentiable-electromagnetic-transients.pdf#page=7)

Author affiliations include Colorado School of Mines. [Publication record](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.jax-emt-differentiable-electromagnetic-transients)

### Label and Evaluation Validity in Machine-Learning Transient Stability Assessment: A Case Study on the IEEE 39-Bus System

2026-10-13 · Preprint · Zenodo

The author simulated 7,000 three-phase faults on the IEEE 39-bus system in ANDES 2.0.0 and reran selected cases with smaller time steps to check the stability labels used to evaluate machine-learning models. [Section 3, Data and labels, opening paragraph and Scenario and Label subsections](https://zenodo.org/records/23337823/files/Label_Evaluation_Validity_TSA_IEEE39.pdf#page=3)

Author affiliations include Power Grid Bangladesh PLC, Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology. [Publication record](https://zenodo.org/records/23337823)

### Vulnerability of data-driven transient stability prediction to cyber-physical attacks: A forensic XAI approach

2026-10 · Journal article · Electric Power Systems Research

The authors ran dynamic simulations in ANDES 2.0.0 on the IEEE 39-bus and WECC 179-bus systems to generate data for assessing the robustness of transient stability predictions. [Test systems and dataset generation, opening paragraph](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0378779626005675)

Author affiliations include Iskenderun Technical University, Texas Tech University, Istanbul Technical University. [Publication record](https://research.itu.edu.tr/en/publications/vulnerability-of-data-driven-transient-stability-prediction-to-cy/)

### PI-Formers: An AI4S Approach for Transient Rotor Angle Prediction in Renewable-Integrated Power System Under Large Disturbances

2026-03-10 · Journal article · IET Renewable Power Generation

The authors used ANDES time-domain simulations to generate post-fault rotor-angle trajectories for training and testing their prediction model on modified IEEE 39-bus systems. [Section 3.2.2, Realization of PI-Formers, step 1 (sample generation)](https://ietresearch.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1049/rpg2.70218)

Author affiliations include Wuhan University, China Electric Power Research Institute, State Grid Hubei Electric Power Research Institute, Colorado School of Mines, University of Denver. [Publication record](https://ietresearch.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1049/rpg2.70218)

### Hybrid deep learning methodology for dynamic stability and instability mode analysis considering renewable penetration

2026-02-12 · Journal article · Electric Power Systems Research

The authors used ANDES to simulate three-phase faults on a modified IEEE 39-bus system with three mixes of synchronous and virtual synchronous generators. They used the resulting data to assess transient stability and identify the dominant instability mode. [Case studies, Basic setup and dataset generation](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0378779626001240)

Author affiliations include State Grid Fujian Electric Power Research Institute, Wuhan University, China Electric Power Research Institute, State Grid Hubei Electric Power Research Institute, Colorado School of Mines, University of Denver. [Publication record](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.epsr.2026.112831)

### Digital-Twin Screening of Grid-Forming Support for Weak Power Systems

2026 · Conference paper · 25th Wind & Solar Integration Workshop

The authors ran reduced positive-sequence simulations in ANDES 2.0.0 to compare grid-forming STATCOM and E-STATCOM support in eastern Denmark. They compared frequency deviations and bus-voltage recovery after a sequence of disturbances. [Section 6.3, Reduced Dynamic Response, Table 5. Figure 6 shows the trajectories](https://vbn.aau.dk/ws/portalfiles/portal/861046992/IET_Conference_DK2.pdf#page=7)

Author affiliations include Aalborg University, Ørsted Wind Systems A/S. [Publication record](https://vbn.aau.dk/en/publications/digital-twin-screening-of-grid-forming-support-for-weak-power-sys/)

### Conservative Q-learning with physics guidance for generator tripping control

2025-11 · Journal article · International Journal of Electrical Power & Energy Systems

The authors ran ANDES on a modified IEEE 39-bus network with three configurations of virtual synchronous generators to evaluate a reinforcement-learning method for generator tripping. [Section 4.1, Basic settings, first paragraph](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S014206152500729X)

Author affiliations include Wuhan University, China Electric Power Research Institute, State Grid Hubei Electric Power Research Institute, Colorado School of Mines, University of Denver. [Publication record](https://jglobal.jst.go.jp/detail?JGLOBAL_ID=202602273932586223)

### Multi-Timescale Modeling Framework of Hybrid Power Plants Providing Secondary Frequency Regulation

2024-11-22 · Journal article · IEEE Open Access Journal of Power and Energy

The authors ran the real-time dynamics of three hybrid power plants in ANDES on a modified IEEE 39-bus system to evaluate how operating modes and secondary frequency regulation affect frequency response and battery dispatch. [Section II-C, RT HPP Control, and Section III, printed p. 600. The case studies appear in Section IV](https://docs.nlr.gov/docs/fy25osti/91511.pdf#page=6)

Author affiliations include Mississippi State University, National Renewable Energy Laboratory. [Publication record](https://research-hub.nlr.gov/en/publications/multi-timescale-modeling-framework-of-hybrid-power-plants-providi/)

### Physics-Informed Fast Transient Stability Assessment of Non-Fixed Length in Power Systems

2024-04-09 · Journal article · Journal of Shanghai Jiaotong University

The authors used ANDES to generate fault trajectories on a modified Kundur system and compared its simulation time with the inference time of their transient-stability assessment model. [Section 3.1, case analysis, and Table 3, timing comparison](https://xuebao.sjtu.edu.cn/article/2025/1006-2467/1006-2467-59-7-962.shtml)

Author affiliations include Wuhan University, China Southern Power Grid Co., Ltd.. [Publication record](https://xuebao.sjtu.edu.cn/EN/abstract/article/1006-2467/47869)

### Real-Time Identification of Electromechanical Oscillations via Deep Learning Enhanced Dynamic Mode Decomposition

2023-09-25 · Conference paper · IEEE Power & Energy Society General Meeting

The authors generated voltage and frequency measurements on a modified Kundur two-area system in ANDES. Figure 3 and Table I](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10252195)

Author affiliations include University of South-Eastern Norway. [Publication record](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10252195)

### Impact of Open Communication Networks on Load Frequency Control with Plug-In Electric Vehicles By Cyber-Physical Dynamic Co-simulation

2023-03-22 · Conference paper · 2023 IEEE Power & Energy Society Innovative Smart Grid Technologies Conference (ISGT)

The authors coupled ANDES transmission dynamics to OpenDSS distribution feeders through HELICS to test how communication delays and packet loss affect frequency regulation by plug-in electric vehicles on a 2,000-bus system. [Section III-A, CPDS Co-simulation Introduction, and Section IV, Case Studies, manuscript p. 3](https://docs.nlr.gov/docs/fy23osti/82369.pdf#page=6)

Author affiliations include National Renewable Energy Laboratory, Mississippi State University, U.S. Department of Energy. [Publication record](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10066380)

[Section IV, Test Power Systems, opening paragraph. Section V, case results](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2020.3040883)

Author affiliations include Grid Protection Alliance, Inc., Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute, University of Tennessee, Knoxville. [Publication record](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2020.3040883)

## Candidates excluded in this review

- **MatPSST: A Matlab/Simulink‐based power system simulation toolbox for research and education**. Section 1 and Table 1 compare ANDES software features. The authors explicitly name Simscape and PSAT as the tools used to verify MatPSST. The results do not report an ANDES run. [Source](https://ietresearch.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1049/gtd2.12805)
- **Communication and Information Security Assessment of a Digital Substation**. The text attributes the use of ANDES to Cui et al. (2018) in its literature review. It does not attribute the present study’s experiments to ANDES. [Source](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/350648159_Communication_and_Information_Security_Assessment_of_a_Digital_Substation)
- **Design and evaluation of multidimensional CNN simulation framework against synchrophasor data spoofing attacks and correction for cyber‐resiliency in power system**. Section 3 gives ANDES and GridDyn only as examples of possible tools. The authors describe their own Python testbed for 25–50 samples per second PMU signals. The only other ANDES occurrence is the bibliography. [Source](https://ietresearch.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1049/gtd2.13348)
- **Rapid system prototype‐based physical simulation platforms for power systems with high penetration of inverter‐based resources**. The platform-design paper mentions ANDES only in the background comparison of existing small research-oriented packages. Its prototype tests its own software and hardware. [Source](https://ietresearch.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1049/hve2.12520)
- **A Review of Safe Reinforcement Learning Methods for Modern Power Systems**. In the benchmarks section the authors list ANDES among available simulators and compare which packages provide grid-forming inverter models. No ANDES experiment was identified. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.00304v2)
- **Cyber-Physical Dynamic System (CPDS) Modeling for Frequency Regulation and AGC Services of Distributed Energy Resources**. The NREL technical report documents ANDES use in Section 3.2.1 and Figure 10. It is a project report. The peer-reviewed papers from the project are indexed separately. [Source](https://docs.nlr.gov/docs/fy22osti/82644.pdf)

## Candidates still awaiting evidence

- **Robust fast PMU measurement recovery enhanced by randomized singular value and sequential Tucker decomposition**. Section 5 identifies a Python-simulation-based 140-bus NPCC case and cites the ANDES methods article as [43]. The text never names the solver used for those data. The separate 39-bus tests used MATLAB/Simulink. No linked study code was found. [Source](https://ietresearch.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1049/gtd2.12520)
- **Robust Local PMU Measurement Recovery Based on Singular Spectrum Analysis of Hankel Structures**. The conference metadata was found, but a body passage or companion code linking this paper’s runs to ANDES could not be verified. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1109/ispec53008.2021.9735614)
- **Cyber-Physical Testbed for Power System Wide-Area Measurement-Based Control Using Open-Source Software**. Section 4.2 describes ANDES, PSAT and GridDyn as interchangeable testbed engines. The WECC case studies do not specify which engine supplied their trajectories. The related 2020 IET article is already indexed for its explicitly documented ANDES latency experiment. [Source](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1803.02337)
- **Distributed Attention-Enabled Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning Based Frequency Regulation of Power Systems**. The HKU deposit verifies tests on 39- and 140-bus systems. The available text does not name the simulator. [Source](https://hub.hku.hk/handle/10722/362817)
- **Distributed Automatic Generation Control Considering DPV Using T&D Dynamic Co-Simulation**. Fang's post about the specific paper confirms reuse of the team's DOE-funded T&D platform. It does not name ANDES. The official NREL record and its linked author publication page do not supply the methods or code. Keep pending. [Source](https://research-hub.nlr.gov/en/publications/distributed-automatic-generation-control-considering-dpv-using-ta/)
- **Distributed optimization and control for autonomous distributed energy resource power dispatch and frequency regulation considering communication failures**. The publisher and lab identify T&D dynamic co-simulation on IEEE 14- and 39-bus systems. The accessible text does not identify the simulator. The publisher says data are available on request. [Source](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2352467726000524)
- **Grid-Aware Assessment of Forced Oscillations from Spatially Distributed Large Loads**. The authors report grid simulations, but the full manuscript does not identify ANDES as their simulator. Shared authorship with a separately verified study does not establish its use here. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.22056v1)
